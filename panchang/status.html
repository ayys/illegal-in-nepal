Month starts vs published BS tables

First day of every solar month from panchanga.month_starts against the lookup tables in nepali-datetime and py-nepali. Rule, as printed in the books: the day runs from one night's midpoint (30 ghati + half the दिनमान after sunrise) to the next, a tie going to the earlier day; a Karka sankranti before sunrise counts for the day before and a Makara sankranti after sunset for the day after. Each era's sankrantis sit slightly off ours (SANKRANTI_ERAS): measured from the 2031–2083 books (for 2024–2040, a separate value per sign), fitted to the tables before 2024. After 2083 the two libraries disagree with each other; the computed dates match py-nepali throughout. A ✓ marks a miss where a book or Drik Panchang confirms the computed date, i.e. the table is wrong (sources in data/month_starts.csv; see docs/VERIFICATION.md). The one open case is 2093/10, a Makara sankranti about 1 pala after sunset, where Drik sides with nepali-datetime.
