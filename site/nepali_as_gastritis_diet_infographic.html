The Healing Himalayan Diet

A specialized nutritional protocol for Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS) and Erosive Gastritis, utilizing traditional Nepali superfoods to bridge the gap between joint inflammation and gastric recovery.

Why This Plan Works

Patients with AS often suffer from gastritis due to NSAID use. This diet uses **low-starch** carbohydrates to starve the *Klebsiella* bacteria (AS trigger) while employing **mucilaginous** foods to physically coat and heal the stomach lining.
